## Not in the files (do not assume)

- Small radio antenna range/power: the SmallBlockRadioAntenna definition has no MaxBroadcastRadius or power field.
- Small camera RaycastConeLimit / RaycastDistanceLimit / RaycastTimeMultiplier: absent for the vanilla small camera (present only on DLC/other cameras).
- Weapon power draw (gatling/autocannon/rocket/railgun): no field in block defs, only the sink group name.
- Programmable block power draw (no power field in its definition); timer power draw (none in the small timer definition).
- How the engine converts MissileHealthPool (railgun) into block damage, and rocket explosion falloff.
- World settings (damage modifiers, inventory multipliers, welding/grinding speed) and gravity.
