- [both] **Merge-block clamp face (unverified).** The small 1x1x1 merge block (`SmallShipSmallMergeBlock`) clamps toward its local **Up** face (one wiki source); the old assumption was Right. Every merge pair in this package is built on that. It is one constant (`MERGE_CLAMP_FACE_SMALL` in `tools/designs/_common.py`); if the merge-face test (T1) shows another face, change it and run `tools/build_all.py` again.
- [both] **Lite variants keep the small merge block.** Its XML shape comes from the Keen source, not from the Starburst reference, so strictly a Lite ship is not 100 % "seen" XML. A drone or printer without it cannot be printed or docked. `LITE_KEEPS_MERGE = False` in `_common.py` drops it from the drones.
- [both] **XML shapes never seen in a game-written blueprint:** Sensor, the AI blocks (Offensive, Flight, Defensive), Decoy, large gatling turret, Timer and Event Controller toolbars. They are why Full variants exist next to Lite ones. Earlier blueprints with a Sensor or Custom Turret Controller failed to paste; the leading hypothesis is a missing `<Toolbar>` element (now always written), but that is not confirmed.
- [both] **Projected Programmable Blocks.** A welded PB may keep its Program text but not start by itself. The printer script calls `ApplyAction("Recompile")` on it; whether that works is the projected-PB test (T5, T6).
- [both] **No ammunition is pre-loaded** in any gun or launcher (the generator cannot write inventories). In a survival world load magazines / rockets by hand; in a creative world the guns may still need magazines unless the world has infinite ammunition switched on (test T8 shows which). Scripts treat an unknown ammo count as unlimited and never return to base for lack of ammo in that case.
- [both] **Scripts have never run in the game with these versions.** They compile against a hand-written stub of the game API (`tools/compile_check.sh`), which proves syntax and types only.
- [both] **Weapon numbers** come from the local game data (gatling 700 rpm, 800 m/s, 800 m; small rocket 500 damage, 4 m blast; small railgun 1000 m/s, 1400 m, 500 ms shot delay). Rockets are assumed not to inherit the shooter's velocity.
- [drone] **Gnat / Midge Lite** thrust only along the nose axis (two axial thrusters, steered by gyros). They have no lateral or vertical thrusters by design.
- [drone] **Wasp** relies on the terminal action `ShootOnce` (name from memory, unverified). If it is wrong the Wasp never fires. Its target-lead solver was checked numerically only.
- [drone] **Midge (experimental)** has no script and no Programmable Block. The AI-block XML is a guess and its settings (attack pattern, selected gun, AI Behavior switch) cannot be pre-set: configure it by hand once, see `Scripts/MidgeNotes.md`. **Midge Lite** is only the Gnat Lite frame renamed and still runs `GnatSwarmAI.cs`.
- [drone] **Basilisk** is a large-grid ship with no merge block; it cannot be printed (paste only). Longbow S / L have no docking face in the base model (printers add a docking stalk to Longbow S and Mantis).
- [drone] Scripts that were forked from the Hornet script (Wasp, Lancer, Harrier, Aegis, Mantis, Basilisk, Watcher) do not share later bug fixes automatically. A drone only joins the hive of another model if both use the same `IGC Tag`.
- [printer] **Fused grid (biggest risk).** Blocks welded from a projection become part of the *projector's* grid. After switching both merge blocks off the drone may still be on the printer's grid. `HiveMotherAI` checks this at run time; if the drone is still on the printer's grid it sets the bay to FAULT with a "FUSED" message and sends no `_JOIN`. The fused-grid test (P1) decides.
- [printer] **Welder reach is modelled, not measured:** the welder's work sphere centre comes from a model dummy we cannot read. v2.1 checks 8 guesses (centre at the tip face or 1 to 3 cells inside the welder, radius 1.3 m or 1.0 m); the outer hull is covered under all of them, deep core blocks (batteries, gyro, remote) only under the shallow ones. If the real centre is deeper, bays stall in FINISH with `N unfinished`, and the LCD lists which blocks.
- [printer] **Hornet-class drones need touching welders.** Hornet, Wasp, Lancer, Watcher, Harrier, Aegis, Mantis and Longbow S are 6 to 7 cells across, so their interior blocks can only be reached by welders whose tips touch the drone surface (a welder tip has no mount point, so no block attaches, but the surfaces do touch). If touching causes a permanent merge, use the Gnat-class printers (Hive Mother, Twin Hive, Quad Hive, Mobile Hive, Static Nest, Stinger Rack), which keep a full cell of air.
- [printer] **No conveyors.** The component stock must sit in a cargo container touching a welder's port, or in the welders themselves. A creative world may not need components at all (unconfirmed). The components needed per drone are listed as comment lines at the top of the PB Custom Data.
- [printer] **Cargo containers** (Full printers) have a guessed shape and volume; the Escort Carrier Full also has a guessed `SmallGatlingTurret`. Lite printers have neither.
- [printer] **Stinger Rack** prints a minimal fallback Stinger (Gnat frame, two warheads, `StingerAI.cs`) because there is no dedicated Stinger design module.
- [printer] Printers are heavy (about 13 to 36 t) and slow to turn; the Static Nest has no thrusters at all (it is still a dynamic grid). Timer / Event-Controller printers ("Midge Nest" and friends) are **not** part of this release.
- [both] **Power budget and run time (computed from `defs_stats.json`, not measured).** Hornet, Wasp, Lancer, Watcher, Harrier, Aegis: 4 small batteries = 0.2 MWh and at most 0.8 MW output; the 12 thrusters could draw 2.4 MW, so a burn on all three axes at once is capped at about 67 % thrust, and a one-axis burn (2 thrusters, 0.4 MW) lasts about 30 minutes. Gnat and Midge Lite: 1 battery = 0.05 MWh and 0.2 MW, i.e. about 15 minutes with the one thruster at full power. Longbow S and Mantis: one 3x2x3 battery = 1 MWh, 4 MW. Longbow L: 12 MWh, 48 MW against 40 MW of thrusters. Power drawn by guns, railgun charging, antenna range and welders is not in the game files, so it is not in these figures. There is no generator or solar panel on any drone: once the batteries are empty the drone is dead weight (`RTB Battery Fraction` sends it home before that).
- [both] **Space only.** Thrust is ion thrust with no gravity term: the best drones accelerate at 6 to 7 m/s2 (Harrier 12 m/s2 forward only), which is less than 1 g (9.81 m/s2), and ion thrusters lose most of their force in an atmosphere. None of these ships can lift off or hover over a planet. Use them in space or at the edge of low gravity. Masses are component masses only; magazines, rockets and cargo add weight.
- [printer] **A printed drone leaves with a nearly empty battery.** The CHARGE phase lasts at most `Max Charge Seconds` (default 25 s). A small battery charges at 0.2 MW, so 25 s adds only about 3 % of its 0.05 MWh; a full recharge of a Hornet's 0.2 MWh from the printer's 4 MW pack takes about 15 minutes. A freshly welded battery starts at its definition's initial charge (30 %, unverified for welded blocks), so expect a drone to leave with roughly one third of its endurance (about 10 minutes of one-axis burn for a Hornet class, 5 minutes for a Gnat). Raise `Max Charge Seconds` in the PB Custom Data if you can wait. Drones that return to the printer are not re-docked or recharged by the scripts.
- [printer] **PCU.** The table's PCU is the printer alone (welders cost 150 each). Every printed drone adds roughly 700 to 1,700 PCU, and every projected block counts 1 PCU while it is projected. A world with a low block limit (survival defaults) will refuse to paste a Quad Hive (8,381 PCU) or Static Nest (6,244 PCU).
